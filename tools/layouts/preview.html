CH 3
Case Study · ITERATION SPEED
Three Engines, Three Tradeoffs
Same brief, three tech stacks, three outcomes.

Unreal

  • Best-in-class lighting out of the box.
  • Compile times kill iteration speed.
  • Best for hero shots, cinematic capture.

Unity

  • Fastest prototype-to-headset.
  • Visual ceiling needs custom shaders.
  • Best for gameplay-first experiences.

WebXR

  • Zero-install onboarding.
  • Feature-detection jungle across browsers.
  • Best for marketing, one-off demos.
